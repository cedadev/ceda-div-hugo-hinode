On Tuesday 7th of July 2026 we will helping facilitate an introduction to high-performance computing course in collaboration with the National Oceanography Centre.

{{< link "https://noc-oi.github.io/2026-07-07-RAL/">}} **Click to register and find out more information about this Carpentries incubator course!** {{</ link >}}

This course is an introduction to using high-performance computing systems effectively and is intended to give you a good introduction to parallel programming and an overview of the tools available & how to use them effectively.

**Who:** This workshop is open to all NERC remit research staff and students who use or have an interest in using JASMIN

**When:** July 7, 2026; 9:30 am - 5:00 pm BST

**Where:** Taking place on-site at RAL:
CR03, Science and Technology Facilities Council, Rutherford Appleton Laboratory, Harwell Campus, Didcot, OX11 0QX - {{< link "https://www.ukri.org/who-we-are/stfc/facilities/rutherford-appleton-laboratory/how-to-get-to-rutherford-appleton-laboratory/">}} **Directions on how to get to site** {{</ link >}}

This workshop is run in collaboration between the National Oceanography Centre and JASMIN. Training accounts will be provided for access to JASMIN.

Please register for this workshop by the 19th of June!
For more information, please email colin.sauze@noc.ac.uk or esther.turner@noc.ac.uk
